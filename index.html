About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at IIT Kharagpur, working with Prof. Ashok Kumar Pradhan and supported by the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship. My thesis, due for submission in December 2026, covers the detection, classification, localization and mitigation of oscillations in power grids with high penetration of converter-interfaced resources.

My work spans electromagnetic transient (EMT) and phasor-domain modelling, synchrophasor (PMU) analytics, and real-time hardware-in-the-loop validation on Speedgoat and OPAL-RT. I am a graduate student member of IEEE and a member of the IEEE Power and Energy Society.

Currently I lead a Grid India (NRLDC) sponsored study on protection performance, online inertia estimation and synthetic inertia requirements for low-SCR renewable pockets of the Northern grid.

Research

Oscillation source identification

Matrix Pencil based frequency estimation and spatiotemporal mode-component ratios to localize forced oscillations from governors, exciters, cyclic loads and HVDC.

System integrity protection

A PMU data-driven, three-stage scheme for controller-level isolation of sustained oscillations, validated on WECC test systems and Indian grid data.

Protection with IBRs

How converter controls cause false distance-relay operation, and a scheme that tells power swings from faults on lines connecting inverter-based resources.

Grid-forming wind control

Adaptive-droop and virtual-inertia control of PMSG wind turbines for frequency support and better damping, validated in real time on Speedgoat.

Data-center load oscillations

ConceFT-based estimation of time-varying forced oscillation frequency injected by AI training loads, tested down to −7 dB noise.

Inertia and weak grids

Online inertia estimation from PMU data, and STATCOM and PLL behaviour at weak-grid solar plants.

Research in operation

The Oscillation Source Identification (OSI) tool reads PMU data from the wide-area measurement system, detects sustained oscillations, estimates their modes and points operators to the source location.
